Privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026 · Applies to the Offernote Android app

Offernote turns recruiting emails in your Gmail into a list of your job applications. It does this on your phone. We do not run a server that receives your emails or your tracker, and the app has no analytics, ads or cloud AI.

In short

What do we read?Only emails that look like recruiting. Others are skipped by their headers.
What do we keep?Company, position, dates, the subject and a short quote. Not the email bodies.
What leaves your phone?Nothing, except requests to the Gmail API and Google sign-in.
How do I stop?Settings › Disconnect Gmail. It deletes everything in the app too.

Access to Gmail

When you connect Gmail, the app asks Google for read-only access (the gmail.readonly scope). It cannot send, delete, label or change your emails. Sign-in and access tokens are handled by Google Play services; the app does not store your password or tokens.

The app searches your mailbox for emails that look like recruiting: application confirmations, invitations, rejections, offers and messages from hiring systems. It checks headers first and skips everything else. The emails it does open are read in the phone's memory to find the company, position, dates and status.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which Android encrypts.

The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. On a new phone, connect Gmail again: the app finds your applications in your email again. Notes and edits you made by hand are kept only in an export file you saved.

When data leaves your phone

  • Gmail API and Google sign-in. The app talks only to gmail.googleapis.com and Google's sign-in services, to search and read the emails described above.
  • Export, only when you choose it: a CSV or JSON file saved where you pick.
  • Add to calendar, only when you tap it: your calendar app opens with the interview details, and you decide whether to save it.
  • Error log and diagnostics, never automatically. The error log is off by default. When you turn it on, the app records the type of an error, where in the app it happened and the app version, never email content, companies or addresses. You can read the whole report on the Diagnostics screen and send it to us yourself by email, or copy it.

Notifications about new updates and interview reminders are created on the phone. No push service is involved.

Google API Services: Limited Use

Offernote's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Gmail data is used only to build and update your job-application tracker on your phone.
  • It is not transferred to us or anyone else, except when you export or share it yourself.
  • It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models.
  • No person reads your emails. We have no copy of them.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Delete all data removes applications, updates, notes, settings and diagnostics from the app. Gmail access is not withdrawn.
  • Settings › Disconnect Gmail and delete data also withdraws the app's access to your Gmail.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is meant for adults looking for a job and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app. A change that sends your data off the phone would need your explicit consent in the app first.
