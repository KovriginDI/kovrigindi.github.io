Your job applications, tracked from Gmail

Offernote is an Android app that finds the recruiting emails in your Gmail and turns them into a list of your applications: where you applied, who replied, when the interview is, and who to follow up with. Everything is read and kept on your phone.

How we handle your data

In a closed beta on Android. To join, write to offernote.app@gmail.com.

What it does

No manual entry

Applications, replies, interviews and offers are found in your email and kept up to date. You can add your own too.

Stays on your phone

Read-only Gmail access. No server, no cloud AI, no analytics. Nothing is uploaded.

Reminders and stats

Know when to follow up, never miss an interview, and see where your search stalls.

Every update shows why

When the app marks an application as rejected or schedules an interview, it shows the line from the email it came from and how sure it is. If it is unsure, it asks you first. Every automatic decision can be fixed in a tap, and your edits always win.

Gmail access is read-only: the app cannot send, delete or change your emails. You can disconnect it and delete everything at any time in Settings.
